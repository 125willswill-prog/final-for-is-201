Bananagrams Solitaire

How to Play:

  • 1. Split: Click "Split!" to receive your starting 15 tiles.
  • 2. Build: Drag the tiles to create a grid of intersecting words (like a crossword).
  • 3. The Double Twist: Don't like your letters? Click "Dump". Any tiles you haven't moved yet will be discarded, but the Robot will replace each one with two new tiles!
  • 4. Peel: Once you've used all your tiles in a valid grid, click "Done" to claim victory!
